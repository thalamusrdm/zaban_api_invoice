# Inventory balances and movement evidence

Source: [Inventory Balances SOP](https://cdn.priority-software.com/docs/SOP_InvBalance_23_E.pdf). The SOP separates online current inventory from recalculated inventory as of a prior date and points to the **Inventory Journal for Warehouse** for transaction-level analysis.

- Define whether the report measures physical on-hand, available/unallocated, movement, or valuation. For valuation specify costing method, date and currency; quantity agreement does not establish valuation agreement.
- Align company, cutoff date/time and warehouse set. Define whether the grain includes bin, lot/serial, status, unit or ownership. Check in-transit and reserved stock separately; never sum quantities across incompatible units.
- Isolate one part and warehouse. Compare opening quantity plus signed movements through the cutoff to the closing balance, using the same inclusion rules. Drill into receipt, shipment, return, transfer, adjustment or count documents for each differing movement. Verify date field, status, source/destination, and conversion into the reporting unit.
- For transfers, expect distinct source/destination effects where applicable. Across a warehouse group, both sides may cancel; that does not verify each warehouse's balance. Late-entered or backdated movements can change an as-of report between runs; record the time of extraction.
- Start with the warehouse journal and the applicable current/as-of balance. Test a transfer and a return/count adjustment when relevant. Do not adjust stock simply to make the report tie out.
